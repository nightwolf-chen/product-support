Privacy Policy — Emoji King GIF (表情帝GIF)

Effective date: September 27, 2026 · Developer: nightwolf-chen · App: Emoji King GIF (iOS, iPhone) · 中文版 · All apps · Contact via GitHub Issues

This Privacy Policy describes how the Emoji King GIF (表情帝GIF) iOS app handles your data. The app is free, requires no account, and stores your favorites on your device. This policy mirrors the app's Apple privacy manifests (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy): tracking is off, nothing is linked to you, and analytics data is not used for tracking.

1. Summary

  • Tracking: No. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and declares no tracking domains. Analytics uses the NO-IDFA variant of the UMeng SDK, so no advertising identifier (IDFA) is involved.
  • Data linked to you: None.
  • Data not linked to you: Device ID and Usage / Performance Data, used for product analytics only — not linked to your identity, not used for tracking, never sold.
  • Not collected: precise location, contacts, health data, account credentials, or purchase history (there are no accounts and no in-app purchases).

3. Analytics

Product analytics is provided by UMeng (NO-IDFA build). Analytics identifiers are used to understand aggregate app usage and improve stability; they are not linked to your identity, not used for tracking, and not sold to data brokers.

4. Advertising

The app is free and shows banner and interstitial ads via Google AdMob. Ad requests go to Google's ad network (including googleads.g.doubleclick.net and googlesyndication.com); Google's handling of ad data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data-use documentation. Where required (e.g. EEA/UK), Google's User Messaging Platform shows a consent form before personalized ads are served.

5. GIF search

Sticker results are fetched live from an image search backend. Your search keywords are sent to that backend to return results; the backend provider's own privacy policy applies to those requests.

6. Stored on your device

Favorites, imports, recent searches, and caches are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers — we operate no user accounts and no backend that receives your personal data. Photos access is used only when you import images or save GIFs, as described in Support. Deleting the app removes this locally stored data.

7. Children

The app is rated 4+ and is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; the analytics and advertising data described above is handled as stated, with nothing linked to the user.

8. Data retention and security

We retain no personal data on our own servers because we collect none directly. Analytics and ad data retained by third-party providers (UMeng, Google AdMob) is subject to their retention policies. Network requests from the app use HTTPS.

9. Your rights

Depending on your region you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data. For data held by third-party providers, please use their privacy controls (see links above). For anything else, contact us via GitHub Issues and we will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted at this same URL and the effective date updated. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
