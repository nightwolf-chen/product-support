Support — Wallpapers (壁纸美图)

Wallpapers for iOS (iPhone, iOS 15+, portrait) · Free with ads · No account required · 中文版

Thanks for using Wallpapers! Answers to common questions are below. If you need more help, contact us via GitHub Issues.

Frequently asked questions

How do I set an image as my wallpaper?

iOS does not let apps change the wallpaper directly. In Wallpapers, open a photo and tap the Save button to save it to your photo library. Then open the Photos app, open the image, tap Share → Use as Wallpaper, adjust and tap Add.

Why does the app ask for photo library access?

Only when you save an image. The app requests add-only access so it can write wallpapers to your library. It never reads or uploads your existing photos.

Why does the app ask for permission to track?

Wallpapers is free and funded by ads. The tracking prompt (App Tracking Transparency) lets you allow personalized ads via Google AdMob. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Details: Privacy Policy.

Search shows no results / loads slowly

Results come live from several image providers over the network. Check your connection, try a simpler keyword (e.g. nature, ocean), and pull to retry. If one provider is down, results from the others still appear.

Where are Discover, Categories, and Favorites?

  • Discover — curated feed with shuffle and infinite scroll.
  • Categories — hand-picked albums (nature, animals, anime, cars, city …).
  • Me / Favorites — your saved collection, offline albums, and cache management.

My favorites disappeared

Favorites are stored only on your device (no account, no cloud sync). Deleting the app or clearing its data removes them permanently — they cannot be restored.

How do I free up storage / clear the cache?

Open the Me tab and use the cache display / clear-cache option. Downloaded full-size images you saved to Photos are managed in the Photos app.

How do I report an inappropriate image?

Images come from third-party providers (Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, Flickr, Openverse, Wikimedia Commons, Bing). Please open an issue with the search keyword or category where you saw it plus a description, and we will review the source query.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone running iOS 15.0 or later, portrait orientation. There is no Android version.

Contact us

Please report bugs or ask questions via GitHub Issues. To help us fix things faster, include:

  • iPhone model and iOS version (e.g. iPhone 15, iOS 18.1)
  • App version (see the Me tab)
  • Steps to reproduce + what you expected
  • A screenshot, if relevant
