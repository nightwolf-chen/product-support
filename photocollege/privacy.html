Privacy Policy — PhotoCollege

Effective date: November 7, 2026 · Developer: Jidong Chen · App: PhotoCollege (iOS) · 中文版 · All apps · Contact via GitHub Issues

This Privacy Policy describes how the PhotoCollege iOS app handles your data. The app is a local photo collage editor; it requires no account.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Photos

Photos you choose are accessed only to build your collage. All image processing (cropping, filters, beautify, export) happens on your device. Exported images are written to your photo library only when you tap Save. The app does not upload your photos.

Local storage

Projects, thumbnails, and exported images are stored in the app’s local container on your device. Deleting the app removes this local data.

Third parties

No third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data-processing SDKs are included.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use GitHub Issues.
