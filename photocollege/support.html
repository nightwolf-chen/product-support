Thanks for using PhotoCollege! Answers to common questions are below. If you need more help, contact us via GitHub Issues.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add photos?

Tap the circular + button below the canvas and choose photos from your library.

Which layouts are available?

Single, Duo, Trio, Grid, Columns, and Rows. Columns and Rows support 2–5 cells. Tap a template to apply it. Extra photos beyond the frame count are hidden; you can drag them back into a frame in Photo order.

How do I reorder photos?

Drag the small handle on a thumbnail in Photo order.

How do I adjust a photo?

Tap Looks to open filters, crop, and beautify. The live preview updates while you change settings.

How do I export a collage?

Tap Export, then Share or Save. The app renders a high-resolution image and saves it to your photo library when you tap Save.

Where are my projects stored?

Locally on your device. PhotoCollege has no account and does not upload your photos.

Why does the app ask for photo access?

To read the photos you select and to save exported collages. All processing is on-device.
