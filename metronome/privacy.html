Privacy Policy — Metronome

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Developer: Jidong Chen · App: Metronome (iOS) · 中文版 · All apps · Contact via GitHub Issues

This Privacy Policy describes how the Metronome iOS app handles your data. The app is a local metronome and guitar practice library; it requires no account.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

Settings, practice history and lesson progress

Your tempo, sound, rhythm, timer and trainer settings are stored locally in the app’s UserDefaults. Practice sessions, goals and guitar lesson progress are also stored locally. Deleting the app removes this data. We cannot access it.

Audio

Metronome clicks and lesson content are generated on your device. The app records no microphone audio and does not send audio anywhere.

Purchases, accounts and advertising

The app has no login, purchases, subscriptions, ads, or tracking. It works offline.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use GitHub Issues.
