Getting started

How do I start the metronome?

Press the round play button on the session card. Press it again to stop. The session timer pauses with playback.

How do I change tempo?

Drag around the circular tempo dial, use the −5 / −1 / +1 / +5 buttons, or tap the BPM number to type an exact tempo. Tap tempo sets the beat from your taps.

What do the practice sessions do?

Every quick session alternates practice and rest. Micro 2 is 1 minute practice + 1 minute break. Focus 5 is 1/1/1/1/1. Blocks 12 is four 2-minute practice blocks with 1-minute breaks. Deep 22 is four 2/1 blocks followed by a 10-minute practice finish. Tap All sessions for Flow 10 and Endurance 30. The click pauses during every break and resumes automatically; only practice time is saved.

How do the guitar courses work?

Open the Learn tab. Two handbooks cover fingerstyle and electric guitar at beginner, intermediate and advanced levels. Each chapter has a tutorial, tab exercise, success criteria and practice plan. Press Start practice to load its tempo, time signature and countdown into the metronome.

Where is my progress stored?

Practice history, goals, settings and lesson progress are stored locally on your device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app clears the data.

Can I use another app while practicing?

Yes. In Settings, enable Mix with other apps. The metronome continues in the background with the screen locked.

I cannot hear the click.

Check the device volume and that no headphones or Bluetooth device is selected. The metronome uses the media/playback audio route, so the silent switch does not mute it. If the problem persists, stop and restart playback.

How do I reset settings or history?

Settings → Reset all settings restores defaults. Progress → Clear history removes saved sessions. Lesson progress is stored separately; contact support if you need help clearing it.

How do I contact support?

Open an issue at GitHub Issues and include your iOS version, device model and a short description.
