Privacy Policy — Wallpapers (壁纸美图)

Effective date: September 27, 2026 · Developer: nightwolf-chen · App: Wallpapers (iOS, iPhone) · 中文版 · Contact via GitHub Issues

This Privacy Policy describes how the Wallpapers (壁纸美图) iOS app handles your data. The app is free, requires no account, and stores your favorites on your device.

1. Summary

  • Tracking: Yes. The app shows personalized ads via Google AdMob and requests App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission to use the device advertising identifier (IDFA) for tracking.
  • Data linked to you: Advertising Data, Device ID (including IDFA), and Product Interaction — used for third-party advertising, developer advertising, and analytics.
  • Data not linked to you: Crash, performance, and diagnostic data (analytics), and the photos you choose to save (app functionality only).
  • Not collected: precise location, contacts, health data, account credentials, or purchase history (there are no in-app purchases).

3. Tracking and advertising

On first launch the app asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency dialog: "This identifier will be used to deliver personalized ads to you."). If you allow it, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is shared with Google AdMob to deliver personalized ads and measure ad performance. Tracking domains used by the ad provider include googleads.g.doubleclick.net and googlesyndication.com.

  • You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Google's handling of ad data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data-use documentation.
  • Where required (e.g. EEA/UK), a consent form is shown before personalized ads are served.

4. Image sources

Wallpaper images are fetched live from third-party image providers, including Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, Flickr, Openverse, Wikimedia Commons, and Bing. Your search keywords are sent to these providers to return results; their own privacy policies apply to those requests. Images are served under each provider's license terms, and attribution is shown on each photo where required.

5. Stored on your device

Favorites, offline albums, recent searches, and caches are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers — we operate no user accounts and no backend that receives your personal data. Deleting the app removes this locally stored data.

6. Children

The app is rated 4+ and is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; advertising and analytics data is handled by the third-party ad provider as described above.

7. Data retention and security

We retain no personal data on our own servers because we collect none directly. Ad and analytics data retained by Google AdMob is subject to Google's retention policies. Network requests from the app use HTTPS.

8. Your rights

Depending on your region you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data. For data held by the ad provider, please use Google's privacy controls. For anything else, contact us via GitHub Issues and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted at this same URL and the effective date updated. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: please open an issue at nightwolf-chen/product-support issues.
